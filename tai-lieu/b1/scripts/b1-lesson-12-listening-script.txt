Narrator: The book of Revelation is the last book of the Bible. John wrote it many years after Jesus rose from the dead. He was on the island of Patmos when he saw a wonderful vision of the future. In his vision, John saw a new heaven and a new earth, because the first heaven and the first earth had passed away.
Friend: What will the new heaven and the new earth be like?
Narrator: John says that God himself will live with his people. He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death will be no more. There will be no more crying and no more pain, because the old things will have passed away. God says, "Behold, I am making all things new."
Friend: That is a beautiful promise. But what about now? We still suffer, and we still cry. How can we have hope today?
Narrator: Peter gives us the answer. He wrote that God has caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead. Our hope is living because Jesus is alive. He has risen, and he will never die again.
Friend: If Jesus had not risen from the dead, would we have any hope?
Narrator: No, we would not. But because he has risen, we have an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading. God is keeping it in heaven for us, and by his power he is guarding us through faith for a salvation that will be revealed in the last time.
Friend: I have been going through a difficult trial this year. Sometimes my faith feels weak.
Narrator: Peter understood that. He said that trials test our faith, and our faith is more precious than gold. When Jesus is revealed, our faith will result in praise and glory and honour. In the new city, we will see the river of the water of life, and the tree of life will bear fruit every month. Night will be no more, and the Lord God will be our light forever.
Friend: I am going to hold on to that hope. I have already seen God's faithfulness in my life, and I know he will finish what he started. One day, I will see him face to face.
Narrator: Yes, and until that day, we rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory, because we know the end of the story. Jesus says, "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end."
