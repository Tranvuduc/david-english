Narrator: Long ago, in the land of Israel, there was a young shepherd named David. He was the youngest of eight sons, and his job was to look after his father's sheep in the fields near Bethlehem.

Narrator: One day, the prophet Samuel came to Bethlehem. God had told Samuel to anoint a new king, and Samuel had already looked at David's seven older brothers. But God had chosen David, the youngest, the shepherd boy. Samuel took his horn of oil and anointed David, and the Spirit of the Lord came upon him.

Narrator: Later, the army of Israel faced a giant named Goliath. For forty days, Goliath had shouted his challenge, and no soldier had dared to fight him. But David said, I will go and fight him. The Lord who saved me from the lion and the bear will save me from this giant.

Narrator: David took his sling and five smooth stones. He ran toward Goliath, and with one stone he struck the giant down. Israel had waited for a hero, and God had given them a shepherd boy with a brave heart.

Friend: That is a wonderful story. But David was not perfect, was he?

Narrator: No, he was not. Many years later, David sinned. He had taken another man's wife, and he had tried to hide his sin. But God sent the prophet Nathan to speak to him, and David repented.

Friend: What did David say to God?

Narrator: He prayed the words of Psalm 51: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me. David knew that God is full of mercy, and he asked God to forgive him and make his heart clean again.

Friend: So David was a man after God's own heart, not because he never sinned, but because he repented?

Narrator: Exactly. When we sin, we can do the same. We can come to God, confess our sins, and trust in His mercy. God had already promised to forgive all who repent and turn to Him.
