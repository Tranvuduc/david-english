Narrator: After Jesus rose from the dead, he met his disciples on a mountain in Galilee. He said to them, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And I am with you always, to the end of the age."
Friend: That is the Great Commission. But I wish I could speak English better. How can I tell people in other nations about Jesus if my English is not good?
Narrator: Jesus also said, "You will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."
Friend: I wish our church had more workers for the harvest. The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few.
Narrator: That is true. But remember Jonah. God sent Jonah to Nineveh, a great city of Gentiles, to preach repentance. Jonah was reluctant. He did not want to go, so he fled to Tarshish, in the opposite direction.
Friend: If Jonah had obeyed God at first, he would not have spent three days in the belly of the great fish!
Narrator: Exactly. But God showed mercy. The fish brought Jonah back to dry land, and Jonah went to Nineveh. The people repented, and God did not destroy the city.
Friend: Jonah was angry, was he not? He wished that God would punish Nineveh.
Narrator: Yes. Jonah said, "I knew that you are a gracious God and merciful, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love." But God asked him, "Should I not pity Nineveh, that great city, in which there are more than 120,000 people?" God's heart is for all nations.
Friend: If the church had sent more workers to Nineveh, more people would have heard the good news sooner.
Narrator: Perhaps. But God can use even a reluctant witness. What about you? Where is God sending you?
Friend: I wish I had more courage. But I will start here, in my own city. I will tell my neighbours about Jesus, and I will pray for the nations.
Narrator: That is a good beginning. Go and make disciples, and remember: Jesus is with you always, to the end of the age.
