Narrator: One day, Jesus sat beside a well in Samaria, and a woman came to draw water. Jesus asked her for a drink, and then he told her about living water, water that gives eternal life. The woman was surprised. She asked Jesus a question about worship. "Our fathers worshiped on this mountain," she said, "but you say that in Jerusalem is the place where people ought to worship." Jesus answered her with words that changed everything. He said, "The hour is coming when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father is seeking such people to worship him. God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth."
Friend: That is a wonderful story. But what does it mean to worship in spirit and truth?
Narrator: To worship in spirit means to worship with a sincere heart, not just with words. To worship in truth means to worship God as he really is, not as we imagine him. We should come to God with a humble heart, and we ought to pray every day. We must remember that God is holy, and we must not worship him in a careless way.
Friend: I want to worship God in the right way, but I do not know how to begin. What should I do first?
Narrator: You could begin with a simple prayer. You might say, "Lord, I come to worship you. You are holy, and I am small." You should thank God for his goodness, and you ought to confess your sins honestly. God might answer your prayer in a way you do not expect, but he always hears you.
Friend: Could you please pray with me now?
Narrator: Of course. Let us bow down and pray together. Lord, we come to worship you in spirit and truth. Help us to be sincere and humble. Teach us to pray every day. In Jesus' name, Amen.
Friend: Thank you. I will pray every morning this week, and I will tell my sister about the living water.
Narrator: That is a wonderful plan. Remember the words of Psalm 95: "Oh come, let us worship and bow down; let us kneel before the Lord, our Maker!"
