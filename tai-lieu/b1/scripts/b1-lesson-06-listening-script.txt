Narrator: Jesus often taught the people with parables, short stories that show what the kingdom of heaven is like. One day, he told them the parable of the sower. A sower went out to sow his seed. Some seed fell along the path, and the birds came and ate it. Other seed fell on rocky ground, where there was not much soil. It grew up quickly, but when the sun rose, it was burned, because it had no root. Other seed fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked it. But other seed fell on good soil, and it gave a harvest, some a hundred times as much, some sixty, some thirty.
Friend: What did Jesus mean by that story?
Narrator: Jesus said that the seed is the word of God, and the soil is the heart of the person who hears it. The good soil is the person who hears the word, understands it, and bears fruit.
Friend: I remember another parable. Jesus said that the kingdom of heaven is like a grain of mustard seed. It is the smallest of all seeds, but when it grows, it becomes the largest of all the garden plants.
Narrator: Yes, and he also said that the kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field. When a man found it, he sold all that he had, and he bought that field. And he said that it is like a merchant who found one pearl of great value, and sold everything to buy it.
Friend: And what about the talents? My teacher told us that a master gave his servants five talents, two talents, and one talent.
Narrator: That is right. The master said that he would return, and he asked his servants to use his money well. The faithful servants worked hard, and they doubled what they had been given. The master was pleased, and he said, Well done, good and faithful servant.
Friend: So the kingdom is worth everything, and God asks us to be ready and faithful.
Narrator: Exactly. Jesus said that we must watch and be ready, because we do not know the day or the hour.
Friend: I love that invitation. I want to share it with my friend this week.
Narrator: That is a wonderful idea. Tell your friend that he is invited to come to God.
