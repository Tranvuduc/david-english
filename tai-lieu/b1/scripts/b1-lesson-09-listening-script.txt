Narrator: The Bible says that the church is like a body. In his first letter to the Corinthians, Paul wrote that just as the body has many members, so it is with Christ. The eye cannot say to the hand, "I do not need you." The head cannot say to the feet, "I do not need you." Every member is important, and every member has a gift from the Holy Spirit.
Friend: I enjoy singing in the choir, but I am not sure my gift is important. Some people preach, and some people teach. I only sing.
Narrator: Paul says that God has given different gifts to different people. Some are apostles, some are prophets, some are teachers. Some have the gift of serving, and some have the gift of encouraging others. There is no small gift in the body of Christ.
Friend: I remember hearing that the church must grow up in love. What does that mean?
Narrator: In his letter to the Ephesians, Paul wrote that Christ gave gifts to his people to build up the body, until we all reach unity in the faith and grow up into maturity. We must learn to speak the truth in love, so that the whole body grows.
Friend: I stopped worrying about my gift when I understood that. Now I want to serve with joy. I decided to help with the Sunday school this year.
Narrator: That is wonderful. Serving together helps us to grow in unity. When we enjoy serving together, the church becomes stronger, and the bond of peace holds us together.
Friend: I also want to learn to encourage my friends. I remember to pray for them every morning, and I try to speak words that build them up.
Narrator: That is exactly what Paul teaches. He says that when each member does its part, the whole body grows and builds itself up in love. We need each other.
Friend: So we are one body, with many members, and one Spirit?
Narrator: Yes. There is one body and one Spirit, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, and one God and Father of all. That is the unity we must keep.
Friend: I will remember to use my gift this week. I want to sing, and I want to encourage the children in Sunday school.
Narrator: That is a wonderful idea. Tell your friends that they are invited to come and to grow with us.
