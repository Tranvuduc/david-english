Narrator: Fifty days after Jesus rose from the dead, the day of Pentecost arrived. The believers were all together in one place in Jerusalem, waiting and praying. Suddenly a sound like a mighty rushing wind came from heaven, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting. Tongues of fire appeared and rested on each one of them. They were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and they began to speak in other tongues, as the Spirit gave them words to speak.
Friend: That must have been amazing! What did the people in the city think?
Narrator: People from many nations were amazed, because each one heard the believers speaking in his own language. Some people were confused, and they asked what it meant.
Friend: What did Peter do?
Narrator: Peter stood up with the eleven apostles, and he spoke out boldly. He proclaimed that Jesus, whom the people had crucified, is both Lord and Christ. His words brought conviction to the crowd, and they asked, What shall we do?
Friend: And what did Peter say?
Narrator: Peter said, Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. For the promise is for you and for your children. About three thousand people were baptized that day, and the church was born.
Friend: That is wonderful. But what happened later, when Peter and John were arrested?
Narrator: The leaders told them not to speak about Jesus anymore. But the believers gathered together and prayed. They asked God to give them boldness to speak His word, while He stretched out His hand to heal and to do signs and wonders.
Friend: Did God answer their prayer?
Narrator: Yes, He did. When they had prayed, the place where they were gathered together was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit again. They continued to speak the word of God with boldness. God had poured out His Spirit on them, and nothing could stop them.
Friend: I want that boldness too. Sometimes I am afraid to speak about Jesus.
Narrator: Then pray like the believers did. Ask God to fill you with His Spirit and to give you boldness. The same Spirit who came at Pentecost still fills God's people today.
Friend: I will. I am going to pray with a friend this week, and I will speak out boldly about Jesus.
Narrator: That is a wonderful decision. Remember the promise: God pours out His Spirit on all people who call on Him.
