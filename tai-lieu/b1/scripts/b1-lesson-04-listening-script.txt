Narrator: Psalm 1 describes the wise person. Blessed is the man who walks not in the counsel of the wicked, nor stands in the way of sinners, nor sits in the seat of scoffers. But his delight is in the law of the Lord, and on his law he meditates day and night. He is like a tree planted by streams of water, that yields its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither. In all that he does, he prospers.
Narrator: Proverbs gives us the same picture of wisdom in many short sayings. Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths. The name of the Lord is a strong tower; the righteous man runs into it and is safe. Proverbs 27 says, iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another.
Friend: I like that picture. Iron sharpens iron. But what does it mean in real life?
Narrator: It means wise friends make us wiser. A friend who tells you the truth, even when it is hard to hear, sharpens you like iron.
Friend: That is true. I have a big decision to make at work this week, and I need wisdom. Should I take the new job in another city, or should I stay here with my family?
Narrator: That is a hard choice. Who have you talked to about it?
Friend: Nobody yet. I was afraid to ask. What if people think I am foolish for even thinking about it?
Narrator: Do not be afraid to ask. The wise person is someone who seeks counsel. So pray first, then talk to a wise Christian friend whose life you respect.
Friend: I will do that. There is an older colleague at work who loves the Lord. I will ask him for counsel tomorrow, and I will pray tonight.
Narrator: That is a wise plan. And remember the lamp: your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. God's word will not show you the whole road at once, but it will show you the next step. Trust him with all your heart.
Friend: Thank you. I feel calmer now. I will take the next step and trust the Lord to make my path straight.
