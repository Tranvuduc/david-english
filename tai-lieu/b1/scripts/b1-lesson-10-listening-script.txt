Narrator: In the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus said, "Do not store up treasures on earth, where moth and rust destroy, and where thieves break in and steal. Store up treasures in heaven instead, where no moth can destroy them. Where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."
Friend: I used to worry about money all the time. Every night, I would check my bank account on my phone, and I would think about tomorrow. Would I have enough? Would I lose my job?
Narrator: Jesus also said, "Do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink. Look at the birds of the air. They do not store up food, but your Father in heaven feeds them. Are you not more valuable than they are?"
Friend: That is hard to believe when the bills arrive. My mother used to say, "Money is like water. It runs away if you do not hold it."
Narrator: The apostle Paul wrote that godliness with contentment is great gain. He said, "We brought nothing into the world, and we cannot take anything out of it. If we have food and clothing, we will be content with that."
Friend: So what should I do? I earn money every month, but I spend it all before the end of the month.
Narrator: First, make a simple budget. Write down what you earn, and plan what you will spend. Save a little every month, even a small amount, and give a little to help others. Be generous, and do not be greedy.
Friend: I have started. This month, I saved some money for the first time. By next year, I will have saved enough for an emergency, and by the year after that, I will have helped my sister with her school fees.
Narrator: That is a good plan. Remember, you cannot serve God and money. Choose your master. Money is a good servant, but it is a bad master.
Friend: I want to learn contentment. I used to think that more money would make me happy, but now I know that enough is enough. My daily bread is enough for today, and tomorrow will worry about itself.
Narrator: Yes. Jesus said, "Do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own." Trust God for today, and let tomorrow wait.
