Narrator: Today we are reading Romans chapter eight. Paul wrote it to Christians who were suffering. Some of them had lost their homes. Some had been put in prison. And Paul had a surprising message for them.
Friend: What did he say? When I am suffering, I usually just want it to stop.
Narrator: Paul said, For I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the glory that is to be revealed to us. Present suffering is real, but future glory is greater.
Friend: That is hard to believe when the suffering is happening now. My mother has been ill for six months, and our family has been going through a very hard time.
Narrator: I am sorry to hear that. Paul does not say that suffering is small. He says that all of creation has been groaning, like a woman in the pains of childbirth, waiting to be set free. We groan inwardly too, as we wait for the redemption of our bodies.
Friend: So what helps us while we wait?
Narrator: The Spirit helps us in our weakness. When we do not know what to pray for as we ought, the Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words. And we know that for those who love God, all things work together for good.
Friend: Even the hard things? Even my mother's illness?
Narrator: Even the hard things. Paul asks, Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword? And he answers: No. In all these things we are more than conquerors through him who loved us.
Friend: If Paul had given up when the suffering came, we would never have read these words.
Narrator: Exactly. And if God had not loved us first, we would have no hope at all. But nothing can separate us from his love. Neither death nor life, nor angels nor rulers, nor things present nor things to come, nor height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation.
Friend: That gives me courage. If my mother had not taught me to pray, I would not know where to turn now.
Narrator: And if you had not trusted God in the hard times, you would not be able to comfort others now. Suffering does not separate us from God. It teaches us that his love holds us, even in the dark.
