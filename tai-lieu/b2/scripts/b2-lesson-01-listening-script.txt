Narrator: Today we are reading a letter from the apostle Peter. He wrote it to Christians who were living far from home, in places all over Asia Minor. They were strangers in those cities. They had no power, no protection, and no permanent home. And Peter had wonderful news for them.
Friend: What did he tell them?
Narrator: He told them that God had caused them to be born again to a living hope. Their inheritance was imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for them. Nothing on earth could destroy it.
Friend: Imperishable, undefiled, and unfading. Those are three big words. What do they mean?
Narrator: Imperishable means it cannot decay. Undefiled means it cannot be made dirty. Unfading means it will never lose its beauty. Our home in heaven is safe, because God himself is guarding it.
Friend: That is beautiful. But what about life here, on earth?
Narrator: Peter calls us sojourners and exiles. A sojourner is a person who is living for a time in a place that is not home. We have been living here for a while, but this world is not our final home. We are like travellers, or pilgrims, on a long journey.
Friend: I understand that. I have been living in this city for five years now, and I have been learning the language and making friends. But sometimes I still feel like a stranger.
Narrator: That is exactly the feeling Peter describes. He says we should abstain from the passions of the flesh, which wage war against our soul. And he says we should keep our conduct honorable, so that other people may see our good deeds and glorify God.
Friend: So our good life is a kind of witness?
Narrator: Yes. Peter says that we are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God's own possession. God chose us so that we may proclaim his excellencies, the wonderful things he has done.
Friend: The first Christians had been waiting for the Messiah for many years before Jesus came. And now we have been waiting for his return for two thousand years.
Narrator: That is right. And while we wait, we live as exiles with hope. Our inheritance is waiting for us, and we are being guarded by God's power through faith.
Friend: I love that. I am a sojourner here, but my home is in heaven.
Narrator: And that hope changes everything. When you know where you are going, you can live well, even far from home.
