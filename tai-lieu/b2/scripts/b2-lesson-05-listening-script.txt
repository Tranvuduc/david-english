Narrator: Today we are talking about leaders in the church. Our friend's church is choosing new elders, and she has questions.
Friend: Yes. Some people want the strongest speaker. Others want the richest man. What does the Bible say a leader should be?
Narrator: Jesus turned leadership upside down. He said: You know that those who are considered rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and their great ones exercise authority over them. But it shall not be so among you.
Friend: What should it be like among us?
Narrator: Whoever would be great among you must be your servant, and whoever would be first among you must be slave of all. For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.
Friend: So the greatest leader is the greatest servant?
Narrator: Exactly. It was Jesus who showed us what leadership means: he washed feet, he carried a cross, and he gave his life. What matters most is not talent or money. What matters most is character.
Friend: What does Paul say about character?
Narrator: The saying is trustworthy: If anyone aspires to the office of overseer, he desires a noble task. Therefore an overseer must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, sober-minded, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, able to teach, not a drunkard, not violent but gentle, not quarrelsome, not a lover of money.
Friend: That is a long list. Why does Paul care about the leader's home life?
Narrator: Because he must manage his own household well, with all dignity keeping his children submissive. For if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how will he care for God's church? What happens at home shows what will happen in the church.
Friend: And what about new believers? Can they be leaders?
Narrator: He must not be a recent convert, or he may become puffed up with conceit and fall into the condemnation of the devil. A leader must also be well thought of by outsiders, so that he may not fall into disgrace, into a snare of the devil.
Friend: So the church should choose servants, not kings.
Narrator: Yes. It is the servant who is truly great, and it is the gentle leader whom God blesses.
