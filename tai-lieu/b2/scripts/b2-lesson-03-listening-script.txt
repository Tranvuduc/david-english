Narrator: Today we are talking about decisions. Our friend has a big one to make, and she does not know what to do.
Friend: I have been offered a job in another city. The pay is good, but I would have to leave my church and my family. I have been praying about it for two weeks, and I still do not know what God wants.
Narrator: Then let us start with Proverbs chapter three. Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths.
Friend: That is easy to say, but hard to do. How do I know what God wants?
Narrator: James gives us the answer. If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach, and it will be given him. God does not scold us for asking. He gives generously.
Friend: I have asked, but I have not heard a clear answer yet. Maybe I need to look at the wisdom from above. James says it is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy and good fruits, impartial and sincere.
Narrator: That is a good test. Ask yourself: is this decision pure? Is it peaceable? Does it show mercy? A decision that comes from God will look like that list.
Friend: What about the manager who interviewed me? He listened to me for an hour before he spoke. He must be a wise man.
Narrator: He must be. A wise person listens first. And what about the job itself? It can't have been an easy offer to make. They must have wanted you very much.
Friend: They did. But I think they might have offered it to someone else if I had said no at once. I asked for a week to pray.
Narrator: That was wise. You must have been praying hard. And remember: if you ask in faith, with no doubting, God will give you wisdom. A double-minded person is unstable in all his ways, but the person who trusts God will find the straight path.
Friend: Then I will do this: I will ask God for wisdom, I will talk to my pastor, and I will wait for peace before I decide.
Narrator: That is the wisdom from above. Pure, peaceable, and gentle. And it will show you the way.
