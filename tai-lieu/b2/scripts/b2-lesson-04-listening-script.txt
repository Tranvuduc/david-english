Narrator: Today we are talking about the mind. Paul says: Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind.
Friend: My mind needs renewing. I wake up and I read the news on my phone, and by nine o'clock I am already worried about everything.
Narrator: It is said that what we feed our minds becomes what we think about. If you feed your mind with bad news all morning, it is no surprise that you feel afraid.
Friend: That is true. But how do I change it? I cannot just stop thinking.
Narrator: Paul gives us a filter in Philippians chapter four. Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence, if there is anything worthy of praise, think about these things.
Friend: So I should ask about every thought: Is it true? Is it lovely? Is it worthy of praise?
Narrator: Exactly. It is thought that our habits shape our minds more than our wishes do. If you get your news from your phone first thing in the morning, your mind is being shaped by the news. If you read God's word first, your mind is shaped by God's word.
Friend: Last week I had my phone repaired, and for two days I had no news at all. I felt so peaceful. I read my Bible in the morning, and I got my work done early.
Narrator: You see? You were transformed by the renewal of your mind. Paul says we should present our bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is our spiritual worship.
Friend: So my mind is part of my worship?
Narrator: Yes. It is said that we become what we think about. Think about what is true, and you will become truthful. Think about what is lovely, and you will become gentle.
Friend: Then I will do this: every morning I will read one chapter of the Bible before I touch my phone, and I will ask: is this thought true, just, pure, and lovely?
Narrator: That is the renewed mind. Do not be conformed to this world. Be transformed.
