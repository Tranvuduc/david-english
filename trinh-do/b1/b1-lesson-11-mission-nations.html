    Bài nghe

    Nghe toàn bộ bài, rồi trả lời 5 câu hỏi bên dưới. Giọng đọc: Casper (người dẫn) và Aria (đối thoại).

    Câu hỏi nghe hiểu

    Câu 1. 1. What did Jesus command His disciples to do on the mountain in Galilee?

    Đáp án: Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them and teaching them to obey everything Jesus commanded.

    Câu 2. 2. What does the Friend wish she could do better?

    Đáp án: She wishes she could speak English better.

    Câu 3. 3. Where would the disciples be witnesses, according to Acts 1:8?

    Đáp án: In Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.

    Câu 4. 4. Why did Jonah flee to Tarshish instead of going to Nineveh?

    Đáp án: He was reluctant and did not want to go, so he fled to Tarshish, in the opposite direction.

    Câu 5. 5. What would NOT have happened if Jonah had obeyed God at first?

    Đáp án: If Jonah had obeyed God at first, he would not have spent three days in the belly of the great fish.

    Câu 6. 6. What did God ask Jonah about Nineveh, and what does it show about God's heart?

    Đáp án: God asked, 'Should I not pity Nineveh, that great city, in which there are more than 120,000 people?' God's heart is for all nations.

    Bài đọc

    After Jesus rose from the dead, He met His disciples on a mountain in Galilee. He said, 'All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.' This is the Great Commission. Jesus also said, 'You will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.' A witness tells others what he has seen and heard. We are His witnesses, first in our own city, then to the nations. The Old Testament already shows God's heart for the nations. God sent the prophet Jonah to Nineveh, a great city of Gentiles, to preach repentance. But Jonah was reluctant. He did not want God to show mercy to Nineveh, so he fled to Tarshish, in the opposite direction. God sent a great fish. After three days, the fish brought Jonah back to dry land, and he went to Nineveh. The people repented, and God showed them mercy. Jonah was angry, but God asked him, 'Should I not pity Nineveh, that great city, in which there are more than 120,000 people?' God's heart is for all nations. God can use even a reluctant witness. Today the harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few. We can begin where we are: tell our neighbours about Jesus, pray for the nations, and go wherever God sends us. Jesus is with us always, to the end of the age.

    Nói

    Roleplay in pairs (3 minutes): Student A is a friend who wishes they could go to another nation as a witness, using wish + past ('I wish I could speak better English', 'I wish our church had more workers'). Student B asks two questions ('Where is God sending you?', 'What would you do if you went?'). Then swap roles and talk about Jonah, using one third conditional ('If Jonah had obeyed at first, he would not have...'). Use at least 4 wish sentences or third conditionals, 5 vocabulary words from Section 3, and contrastive stress on the key word in at least 3 sentences (I love YOU / I LOVE you / ALL nations / my WITNESSES).

    Viết

    Write a short paragraph (80-100 words) about where God is sending you as a witness. Begin: 'God is sending me to...' Use at least 2 wish + past sentences, 1 third conditional, and 5 vocabulary words from Section 3.

    Đọc hiểu

    Câu 1. 1. Why can Jesus send His disciples to all nations?

    Đáp án: Because Jesus said, 'All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me,' and on that authority He sends His disciples to all nations.

    Câu 2. 2. What are the disciples told to do in Matthew 28:19-20?

    Đáp án: To make disciples of all nations, baptizing them and teaching them to obey everything Jesus commanded.

    Câu 3. 3. According to Acts 1:8, what will happen when the Holy Spirit comes upon the disciples?

    Đáp án: The Holy Spirit gives them power, and they will be His witnesses in Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.

    Câu 4. 4. Why did Jonah flee to Tarshish instead of obeying God?

    Đáp án: He was reluctant. He did not want God to show mercy to the people of Nineveh, so he fled to Tarshish.

    Câu 5. 5. What happened when Jonah finally preached in Nineveh?

    Đáp án: The people repented, and God showed them mercy and did not destroy the city.
