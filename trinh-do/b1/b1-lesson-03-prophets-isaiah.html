Passive voice (present/past simple passive; passive with by)

    Phát âm

    Weak forms: of, to, and, have in connected speech — phần luyện phát âm chi tiết nằm trong file PDF bài học.

    Đoạn Kinh Thánh của bài: Isaiah 53; Isaiah 55

    Bài nghe

    Nghe toàn bộ bài, rồi trả lời 5 câu hỏi bên dưới. Giọng đọc: Casper (người dẫn) và Aria (đối thoại).

    Thời lượng: 2:20 · 349 từ · Xem transcript (TXT)

    Câu hỏi nghe hiểu

    Câu 1. 1. How many years before Jesus was Isaiah a prophet?

    Đáp án: About 700 years before Jesus was born.

    Câu 2. 2. True or False: Isaiah 53 was written after Jesus came.

    Đáp án: False — it was written long before Jesus came.

    Câu 3. 3. According to Isaiah 53, why was the Servant wounded and crushed?

    Đáp án: For our transgressions and our iniquities — He took our place.

    Câu 4. 4. According to Isaiah 55, what does God invite thirsty people to do, and what does it cost?

    Đáp án: To come to the waters (come to God); it is free — without money and without cost.

    Câu 5. 5. What does Isaiah 55 say we should do, and what will God do in return?

    Đáp án: Seek the Lord, call on Him, forsake our wicked ways and return to Him; He will have mercy and freely pardon.

    Nói

    Roleplay in pairs (3 minutes): Student A invites Student B to church using Isaiah 55:1 ('Come, all you who are thirsty...'). Student B asks two questions. Use at least 3 passive sentences (e.g. 'You are invited...', 'Forgiveness is given freely...', 'We are welcomed by God...') and 4 vocabulary words from Section 3.

    Viết

    Write an invitation message (80-100 words) to a friend who is far from God, based on Isaiah 55:1-7. Use at least 3 passive sentences and 4 vocabulary words from Section 3. Begin: 'Dear friend, I want to share some wonderful news with you...'

    Đọc hiểu

    Câu 1. 1. When did Isaiah live, and who was he?

    Đáp án: About 700 years before Jesus; a prophet who spoke God's word to Israel.

    Câu 2. 2. What did the Servant suffer, according to Isaiah 53:5?

    Đáp án: He was wounded for our transgressions and crushed for our iniquities.

    Câu 3. 3. What does 'by His wounds we are healed' mean?

    Đáp án: Because Jesus suffered in our place, we can be forgiven and have peace with God.

    Câu 4. 4. How is God's invitation in Isaiah 55 different from buying something in a shop?

    Đáp án: It is completely free — no money, no cost; forgiveness is given by grace.

    Bài tập về nhà

    Viết: A. Rewrite these 5 active sentences in the passive: 1) God called Isaiah to be a prophet. 2) The soldiers pierced Jesus on the cross. 3) People rejected the Servant. 4) God offers living water to the thirsty. 5) The Lord forgives all who return to Him. B. Write 6 sentences about Isaiah 53 using the passive voice (use: wounded, crushed, despised, rejected, pierced, laid).

    Nghe: Listen to the audio again. Fill in the missing words: 'He was ______ for our transgressions. He was ______ for our iniquities. The punishment that brought us ______ was on him, and by his ______ we are healed.' Then answer: What did the Friend decide to do this week?

    Câu ghi nhớ: He was pierced for our transgressions; He was crushed for our iniquities; upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed. — Isaiah 53:5 (ESV). Also learn Isaiah 55:1: Come, everyone who thirsts, come to the waters.
