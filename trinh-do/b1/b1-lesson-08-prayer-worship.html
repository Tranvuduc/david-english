    Bài nghe

    Nghe toàn bộ bài, rồi trả lời 5 câu hỏi bên dưới. Giọng đọc: Casper (người dẫn) và Aria (đối thoại).

    Thời lượng: 2:21 · 365 từ · Xem transcript (TXT)

    Câu hỏi nghe hiểu

    Câu 1. 1. Where did Jesus meet the Samaritan woman?

    Đáp án: Beside a well in Samaria.

    Câu 2. 2. What did the woman ask Jesus about?

    Đáp án: Where people ought to worship — on the mountain or in Jerusalem.

    Câu 3. 3. What did Jesus say true worshipers must do?

    Đáp án: They must worship the Father in spirit and truth.

    Câu 4. 4. What does “worship in spirit” mean, according to the narrator?

    Đáp án: To worship with a sincere heart, not just with words.

    Câu 5. 5. What might God do when we pray?

    Đáp án: He might answer our prayer in a way we do not expect — but he always hears us.

    Câu 6. 6. What did the Friend decide to do this week?

    Đáp án: She decided to pray every morning this week, and to tell her sister about the living water.

    Bài đọc

    Psalm 95 begins with a joyful invitation: “Oh come, let us sing to the LORD; let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation!” Then it gives the reason: the Lord is a great God and a great King above all gods. He made the sea and the dry land, and we are the people of His pasture, the sheep of His hand. So the psalm says, “Oh come, let us worship and bow down; let us kneel before the LORD, our Maker!” True worship begins with knowing who God is — and who we are. We should come to Him with a humble heart, because He is holy and we are small. We ought to thank Him for His goodness before we ask Him for anything. And we must remember that worship is not only singing; it is a whole life offered to God. In John 4, Jesus met a Samaritan woman at a well. She asked Him where people ought to worship — on the mountain or in Jerusalem. Jesus answered that true worshipers must worship the Father in spirit and truth. To worship in spirit means to worship with a sincere heart, not just with words or a ceremony. To worship in truth means to worship God as He really is, not as we imagine Him. The Father is seeking such people to worship Him. God might answer our prayers in a way we do not expect, but He always hears us. So we should pray every day, we ought to confess our sins honestly, and we must never worship with a proud heart. When we come to God in spirit and truth, we find what the woman at the well found: living water, and a Saviour who knows us completely and loves us still.

    Roleplay in pairs (3 minutes): Student A is a new believer who wants to learn how to pray. Student B is a friend who helps. Student B gives advice with should and ought to (“You should pray every day”, “You ought to confess your sins honestly”), and talks about possibility with might and could (“God might answer in a way you do not expect”). Student A makes two polite requests with rising intonation (“Could you please pray with me?”) and gives one command with falling intonation (“Kneel down and pray with me.”). Use at least 5 vocabulary words from Section 3.

    Đọc hiểu

    Câu 1. 1. According to Psalm 95, why should we worship the Lord?

    Đáp án: Because the Lord is a great God and a great King, and He made us — He is our Maker.

    Câu 2. 2. According to John 4:20, what did the Samaritan woman ask Jesus?

    Đáp án: Where people ought to worship — on the mountain or in Jerusalem.

    Câu 3. 3. According to John 4:24, how must those who worship God worship Him?

    Đáp án: They must worship the Father in spirit and truth.

    Câu 4. 4. According to John 4:23, whom is the Father seeking?

    Đáp án: The Father is seeking such people to worship Him.

    Câu 5. 5. According to John 4:25–26, what did the woman say, and how did Jesus answer?

    Đáp án: The woman said, “I know that Messiah is coming,” and Jesus answered, “I who speak to you am He.”
