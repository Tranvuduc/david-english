    Bài nghe

    Nghe toàn bộ bài, rồi trả lời 5 câu hỏi bên dưới. Giọng đọc: Casper (người dẫn) và Aria (đối thoại).

    Câu hỏi nghe hiểu

    Câu 1. 1. What happened on the day of Pentecost?

    Đáp án: With a sound like a mighty rushing wind, and with tongues of fire that rested on each believer. They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues.

    Câu 2. 2. What did Peter do on the day of Pentecost?

    Đáp án: He stood up and proclaimed that Jesus, whom the people had crucified, is both Lord and Christ — and he called the people to repent and be baptized.

    Câu 3. 3. How many people were baptized on the day of Pentecost?

    Đáp án: About three thousand people were baptized that day.

    Câu 4. 4. What happened when the believers prayed together in Acts 4?

    Đáp án: The place where they were gathered together was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit again.

    Câu 5. 5. What did the believers ask God for in their prayer?

    Đáp án: They asked God to give them boldness to speak His word, while He stretched out His hand to heal and to do signs and wonders.

    Câu 6. 6. What did the Friend decide to do this week?

    Đáp án: He decided to pray with a friend this week and speak out boldly about Jesus.

    Bài đọc

    Fifty days after Jesus rose from the dead, the day of Pentecost arrived. The believers were all together in one place in Jerusalem, waiting and praying as Jesus had told them to do. Suddenly, a sound like a mighty rushing wind came from heaven and filled the whole house where they were sitting. Tongues of fire appeared and rested on each one of them. The Holy Spirit came upon them, and they were all filled with power. They began to speak in other languages, as the Spirit gave them words to speak. People from many nations were amazed, because each one heard the believers speaking in his own language. Some people were confused, but Peter stood up with the eleven apostles and spoke out boldly. He proclaimed that Jesus, whom the people had crucified, is both Lord and Christ. His words brought conviction to the crowd. They asked, "What shall we do?" Peter said, "Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit." About three thousand people were baptized that day, and the church in Jerusalem was born. Soon after this, Peter and John were arrested and threatened, and the leaders told them not to speak about Jesus anymore. But the believers did not hide. They gathered together and prayed: "Lord, give your servants boldness to speak your word." As they prayed, the place where they were gathered was shaken. They were filled with the Holy Spirit again, and they continued to speak the word of God with boldness. The same Spirit who came at Pentecost still fills God's people today, and He still gives them power to be His witnesses.

    Đọc hiểu

    Đáp án: The Holy Spirit came upon the believers, and they were filled with power and began to speak in other languages.

    Câu 2. 2. What did Peter do when he stood up before the crowd?

    Đáp án: He stood up and proclaimed that Jesus is Lord and Christ, and he called the crowd to repent and be baptized.

    Câu 3. 3. What was the result of Peter's sermon?

    Đáp án: About three thousand people received his word and were baptized that day.

    Câu 4. 4. What did the believers do in Acts 4 after Peter and John were threatened?

    Đáp án: They were filled with the Holy Spirit again and continued to speak the word of God with boldness.
